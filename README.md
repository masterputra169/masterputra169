## About

I'm **Timotius Kusuma Wardana Putra**, a Web3 developer from Indonesia who came up through the market side of crypto, with four years as **trader → airdrop hunter → alpha hunter → builder**. That path shapes how I build: wallet-native products that feel simple for normal people, and trading systems that have to survive contact with live markets.

- **Building:** [DollarKilat](https://dollarkilat.xyz), get paid in USDC and spend through QRIS, on Solana
- **Researching:** BTC price forecasting, XGBoost vs LSTM on technical + on-chain features
- **Exploring:** Hyperliquid perps, game theory in prediction markets, Web3 payment rails
- **Ask me about:** dApps · DeFi · prediction markets · quant automation
